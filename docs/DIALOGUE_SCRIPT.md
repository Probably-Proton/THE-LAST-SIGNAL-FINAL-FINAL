## 1. Safety & Content Warning (Mandatory Review)

> [!WARNING]
> **CONTENT SAFETY AUDIT FOR SCHOOL AUDIENCE (CLASSES 6–12):**
> 1. **Threatening Phrase in Puzzle 5:** The proposed script includes an intercepted Morse phrase: `'You are next to die.'` For a school environment, this phrasing poses serious safeguarding risks. It must be framed strictly as fictional historical radio drama or replaced with an age-appropriate narrative phrase (e.g. `'THE ARCHIVE IS NOT SAFE'` or `'THE SIGNAL CANNOT BE ERASED'`) before live release.
> 2. **Real-World Geographic Coordinates:** The script references specific real-world coordinates ($37.78857^\circ\text{ N}, -122.45605^\circ\text{ W}$). The interface must continuously reinforce that all gameplay is digital and simulated, strictly prohibiting physical visits to real street intersections.

## 3. Optional Reactive Messages

These messages are designed to be conditionally injected by the investigation dispatch system based on player actions:

* **Incorrect Answer Submitted:**
  * **Sender:** `ZODIAC`
  * **Text:** *"A confident answer. Shame it arrived before the evidence."*
  * **Trigger:** Dispatched after 2 consecutive failed submission attempts on any stage terminal.
* **Hint Requested:**
  * **Sender:** `DIRECTOR OF POLICE`
  * **Text:** *"Return to the records you have already confirmed. One of them may be doing more than one job."*
  * **Trigger:** Dispatched when a player opens the Clue Docket / Hint Modal.
* **Early Solve:**
  * **Sender:** `ZODIAC`
  * **Text:** *"Well, that was quicker than expected. Don't let it make you careless."*
  * **Trigger:** Dispatched if a stage is cleared within the first 6 hours of opening.
* **Quiet Day (Inactivity):**
  * **Sender:** `DIRECTOR OF POLICE`
  * **Text:** *"The case remains open. Review your notes and check whether any clue has been left unconnected."*
  * **Trigger:** Dispatched if an investigator has not logged an action in 24 hours while a stage is open.
* **Near the Finale (Stage 6/7 Transition):**
  * **Sender:** `ZODIAC`
  * **Text:** *"The pieces are close enough to touch. Are you sure you have placed them correctly?"*
  * **Trigger:** Dispatched upon entering the final stage sequence.

### Verified Original Monologue (from `The_Last_Signal_Organized.pdf`, Page 6):
```text
"Four locations. One has been chosen. Three are irrelevant. You have twenty-four hours to tell me which one. 

Don’t mistake this for a game, detectives. I know you’ve been studying my words, searching for patterns, convincing yourselves that every little detail brings you closer to me. But what happens when your instincts fail you? When the clock reaches zero and you realize you sent your attention to the wrong place? 

Choose carefully. I won’t give you a second chance. One wrong answer. One more name on your list. 

Tick-tock, detectives. Someone is counting on you."
```
* **Speaker:** The Antagonist / Killer Voice.
* **Audio Characteristics:** Gritty, telephone line bandpass filter (300Hz–3400Hz), subtle background electromagnetic interference and faint periodic beeps.
* **Monologue Duration:** Exactly 30 seconds (00:00 to 00:30).
* **Terminal Section (00:30 to 00:47):** 17 seconds of rhythmic high-frequency pulse anomalies containing the reversed coordinate audio.
